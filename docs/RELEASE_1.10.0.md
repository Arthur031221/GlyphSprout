# GlyphSprout 1.10.0

The Characters stage can now add accented Latin letters in one click. **Accented lowercase** and **Accented uppercase** append the precomposed letters that Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and many other languages use, such as e with an acute accent, n with a tilde, u with a diaeresis, c with a cedilla and the German sharp s.

Each button adds only the characters your text is missing, and the result stays a draft until **Project ready**. All presets together still fit the 512 glyph limit. The button labels are available in the nine interface languages.

Try the [live workshop](https://Arthur031221.github.io/GlyphSprout/) or follow the [user guide](https://github.com/Arthur031221/GlyphSprout/blob/main/docs/USAGE.md).
