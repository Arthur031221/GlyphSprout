# GlyphSprout 1.27.0

Curly quotes and apostrophes now type with the glyphs of your straight apostrophe and double quote. Text pasted from a phone or a word processor no longer shows those marks as missing, in the live preview or in the exported TTF and WOFF2 files.

Try the [live workshop](https://Arthur031221.github.io/GlyphSprout/) or follow the [user guide](https://github.com/Arthur031221/GlyphSprout/blob/main/docs/USAGE.md).
