# GlyphSprout 1.4.0

Reviewing a full font is quicker. The glyph list shows how many glyphs you have reviewed, **Next to review** jumps to the next one that still needs a check, and the arrow keys move between glyph tiles.

A new Font spacing panel sets the word space for the whole font. Until now every exported font used the same space width. You can also copy one glyph's Latin side bearing to every Latin letter in a single undoable step. The word space is stored in project backups, and older backups open unchanged with the previous width.

**Download specimen image** saves your preview text as a PNG, ready to share. Characters without a glyph are left out of the image.

All new text is available in the nine interface languages, and the demo recording shows the spacing change.

Try the [live workshop](https://Arthur031221.github.io/GlyphSprout/) or follow the [user guide](https://github.com/Arthur031221/GlyphSprout/blob/main/docs/USAGE.md). Exported fonts remain simple unhinted outlines without kerning or connected script shaping.
